# Privacy Policy for Oblivion

*Last Updated: August 7, 2026*

Welcome to **Oblivion** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Oblivion application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
Oblivion is designed as a **privacy-first, local-first application**. The following data is processed directly inside your browser's `LocalStorage` and remains on your device unless you explicitly sign in to sync with cloud services:
- Custom tasks and checklist items
- Personal notes and canvas node diagrams
- Pomodoro streak histories and focus statistics
- Hydration logs and water reminder preferences
- Sound/rain volume preferences and background selections

### B. Account Information (Firebase Authentication)
When you choose to sign in to Oblivion:
- We receive basic profile credentials via Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In (such as your name, email address, and avatar image URL).
- Firebase assigns a unique user identifier (`UID`) to scope your synced tasks and calendar preferences securely.

### C. External Integrations & OAuth Data
- **Google Calendar Integration**: If connected, we process read-only calendar events directly in memory to display your schedule. We do not sell or store your calendar events on external servers.
- **Spotify Integration**: If connected, we use your authorization token to fetch and play ambient ambient playlists. Spotify credentials are managed securely via OAuth tokens.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to:
- Provide core focus, productivity, audio synthesis, and ambient visuals.
- Synchronize your tasks, events, and focus stats across your authorized devices.
- Deliver automated desktop and browser hydration/water break reminders.
- Maintain application reliability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Local Storage**: You can clear all locally stored preferences, notes, and tasks at any time by clearing your browser site data or clicking "Clear Local Storage" in Settings.
- **Cloud Account Data**: If you sign in, your data remains stored securely in Firebase Firestore. You can request complete deletion of your account and associated data by emailing `info.cometlabs@gmail.com`.

---

## 4. AI & Third-Party Service Providers

- Oblivion does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or prompts.
- AI-generated features (such as quote generators or intelligent task categorization) process requests via server-side API endpoints without storing personal user identities.
