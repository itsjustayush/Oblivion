Oblivion — Ambient Minimalist Pomodoro & Focus Workspace

Oblivion is an ambient, minimalist Pomodoro study timer and deep work workspace. Built for students, developers, writers, and knowledge workers, Oblivion combines distraction-free timekeeping, native Spotify audio playback, background soundscapes, Google Calendar and Tasks synchronization, and daily streak analytics.

How does the Pomodoro focus timer in Oblivion work?

Oblivion applies the proven Pomodoro Technique to help you maintain cognitive stamina and avoid burnout during intensive study or work sessions. The workspace structures your day into focused effort sprints balanced by regular restorative breaks:

  • Standard Pomodoro: 25 minutes of uninterrupted deep focus followed by a 5-minute restorative short break.
  • Deep Work Sprint: 50 minutes of sustained concentration followed by a 10-minute mental break, ideal for programming and writing.
  • Long Break Cycles: An extended 15-minute or 30-minute pause every four completed Pomodoro cycles to recharge focus.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press Space to play/pause the timer, R to reset the current interval, S to skip forward to the next session, and F to toggle immersive fullscreen mode.
  • Acoustic Alerts: Gentle Web Audio chimes provide subtle auditory cues when cycles complete, eliminating jarring phone notifications.

How does Spotify integration work in Oblivion?

Oblivion includes native Spotify integration through a secure server-side OAuth proxy and the Spotify Web Playback SDK. You can control playback without leaving your focus environment:

  • Tab-Free Music Control: Play, pause, skip, and adjust track volume directly on the Oblivion dashboard.
  • Curated Focus Playlists: One-click access to curated ambient study, lo-fi hip hop, synthwave, classical piano, and deep binaural beats.
  • Personal Library Streaming: Stream personal saved playlists, albums, and liked tracks seamlessly.
  • Soundscape Blending: Layer Spotify music alongside built-in nature sounds like rain or coffee shop chatter for personalized audio focus.

How does Google Calendar and Google Tasks synchronization work?

Oblivion connects with Google Workspace APIs under client-side authorization, offering a unified hub for daily productivity:

  • Agenda Overview: View today's Google Calendar events and scheduled meetings without opening separate calendar windows.
  • Two-Way Tasks Sync: Pull Google Tasks directly into Oblivion, create new to-do items, and mark tasks as completed in real time.
  • Privacy First: OAuth tokens are maintained safely on the client and never stored on third-party servers.

What ambient soundscapes and background environments are available?

Oblivion features six high-definition looping atmospheric audio generators designed to mask harsh room noise and enhance concentration:

  • Gentle Rain: Soft rainfall drumming against glass windows.
  • Distant Thunderstorm: Deep rumbling thunder and rolling precipitation.
  • Cozy Parisian Cafe: Warm background acoustic chatter, espresso cups, and ambient cafe life.
  • Crackling Campfire: Warm glowing wood embers and crackling hearth sounds.
  • Gentle Ocean Waves: Rhythmic coastal surf and relaxing tidal flow.
  • Calming White Noise: Evenly balanced frequencies for blocking distractions in busy open offices.

How does Oblivion protect user privacy and manage data storage?

Oblivion is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture. It does not display advertisements, track third-party cookies, or monetize user data. All session records, tasks, and settings are saved in client-side localStorage by default. When signed in through Google Firebase, data is encrypted and governed by strict Firestore security rules scoped exclusively to your verified user ID.

Is Oblivion available offline as a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

Yes. Oblivion is fully equipped with a modern service worker and Web App Manifest (/site.webmanifest). The timer, sound generators, and local task manager remain operational even without an active internet connection. You can install Oblivion directly onto your desktop or mobile home screen for an app-like experience.

How do productivity analytics and session streaks work?

Oblivion automatically logs every completed focus interval. You can review your daily productive minutes, weekly focus distribution, session completion ratios, and consecutive day streak counts to foster long-term study habits.
